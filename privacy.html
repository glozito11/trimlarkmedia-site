Privacy Policy

Trimlark Poster is an internal tool run by Trimlark Media to send short videos to TikTok accounts that Trimlark Media operates. Last updated: November 19, 2026.

Who uses it

Only Trimlark Media's own TikTok accounts. The app is not offered to the public, and no other person's account is connected to it.

What we receive from TikTok

  • The access and refresh tokens TikTok issues when an account owner authorizes the app (scope video.upload).
  • The account's open ID and display name, so we know which account a video goes to.
  • The upload status TikTok returns for each video we send.

We do not request or store followers, messages, viewing history, contacts, location or payment information.

How we use it

Only to upload a video to the authorized account's TikTok inbox, where the account owner reviews and posts it in the TikTok app. Nothing is posted publicly by the app itself.

Storage and sharing

Tokens are stored in a private, owner-only file on Trimlark Media's own computer and are used only by the posting tool. We do not sell, rent or share any TikTok data with anyone. We do not use it for advertising or profiling.

Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept while the account stays connected. Revoking the app in TikTok (Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services) disconnects it immediately; we then delete the stored tokens. To ask for deletion, email privacy@trimlarkmedia.com.
